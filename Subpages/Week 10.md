## 🎯 Learning Objectives
Complete the OWASP Top 10 curriculum by mastering SSRF, Integrity Failures, and Logging/Monitoring gaps.

## 📖 Key Topics
1. **Software and Data Integrity Failures (A08:2021)**: Insecure deserialization, unverified auto-updates, CI/CD pipeline vulnerabilities.
2. **Security Logging and Monitoring Failures (A09:2021)**: Log tampering, insufficient audit logs, delayed incident response detection.
3. **Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF) (A10:2021)**: Internal service enumeration, cloud metadata endpoint exploitation (169.254.169.254).
