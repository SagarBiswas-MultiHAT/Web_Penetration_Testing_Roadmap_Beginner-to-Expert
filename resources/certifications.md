## 🎁 Free & Budget-Friendly Training & Badges

If you're on a tight budget, these verifiable credentials and free learning paths provide massive value:

### 1. **ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)**
- **Cost**: **$0** (ISC2 One Million Certified in Cybersecurity Initiative)
- **Includes**: Free official online self-paced training and first exam voucher.

### 2. **TryHackMe Learning Path Certificates**
- **Cost**: Free tier / ~$14/mo Premium
- **Paths**: Web Fundamentals, Jr Penetration Tester, Offensive Pentesting, SOC Level 1
- **Certificate**: Verifiable completion badges for resume and LinkedIn.

### 3. **EC-Council CodeRed Free Courses**
- **Cost**: **$0**
- **Includes**: ~24 micro-courses covering SQLi, Cloud Security, Python Hacking with verifiable certificates.

### 4. **PortSwigger Web Security Academy Badges**
- **Cost**: **$0** (Completely Free)
- **Includes**: Practitioner and Certified Web Security Practitioner certifications directly from the creators of Burp Suite.

### 5. **Microsoft Learn & Cisco Networking Academy**
- **Cost**: **$0**
- **Badges**: Microsoft Security Fundamentals, Cisco Junior Cybersecurity Analyst.

## ⚖️ General Exam Preparation Tips

1. **Practice Over Theory**: For practical exams like OSCP, eJPT, and eWPT, spends 80% of your prep time solving lab machines (TryHackMe, HackTheBox, VulnHub, Proving Grounds).
2. **Master Report Writing**: OSCP and OSEP require a professional penetration testing report. Practice writing clean, executive-ready reports during your lab practice.
3. **Cheat Sheets & Notes**: Keep a well-organized Obsidian or Notion notebook with one-liner command references (Nmap, Burp, SQLMap, privilege escalation scripts).
